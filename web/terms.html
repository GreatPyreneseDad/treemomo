Play safely and kindly

  • Stay on sidewalks, parks and public ground, or places you have permission to be. Don't climb or damage trees.
  • Don't film people up close or without their OK. Only film hugs of people who agree to it.
  • Cleanups are for litter that's safe to pick up by hand or with a bag. Never touch needles, broken glass, chemicals or dumping, and never step into traffic. Report those to your city.
  • Notes you leave must be kind and safe for all ages: no hate, harassment, personal information, links or ads.

Tree health

Health notes are observations from a short video made by an AI model. They are not an arborist's diagnosis. Talk to a certified arborist about a tree you're worried about.

Your content

You keep your videos. You let TreeMomo store and analyze them to run the game, and show the tree, its Momo, the moment and your public notes to other players.
