Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026

TreeMomo is a game: you film real trees, find the Momo living in each one, and help look after trees. This page says what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Tree videos you film in the app, and still frames from them. They're stored on Google Cloud and analyzed by Google's Gemini models to identify the tree, its health, and where its Momo hides.
  • Your location when you film, so the tree goes on the map, the Momo gets a name from its neighborhood, and the moment gets the local weather (from Google Maps Platform).
  • A random player ID stored in your browser, so your journal works without an account.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, used only to keep your Momos, cleanups, hugs and badges with you across devices. We don't get your Google password or anything else in your account.
  • Notes you leave on a tree (memories), with the name you choose, if any. These are public to other players.

Hug clips

Clips of you or a friend hugging a tree are checked by Gemini and deleted right after the check, whether or not the hug counted. We keep only that a hug happened.

What other players see

Trees, their Momos, their health notes, the chronicle of moments, cleanups and hugs (without names), and memories people chose to leave. Your email is never shown. Please don't film people up close.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. Sponsor notes on Momos are offers from nearby places, not tracking.

Deleting your data

Email macgregortechnologies@gmail.com from your account's address and we'll delete your account, your videos and your journal. Trees and their Momos stay on the map for everyone.
